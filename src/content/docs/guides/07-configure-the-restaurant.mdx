Any access granted by the restaurant lets you **read** its configuration, without any scope. **Changing** it needs the `restaurant:manage` scope and a read and write API key. See [Permissions](/partnership-documentation/guides/04-list-restaurants#permissions-scopes).

## Special Options

Special options are the choices a guest makes when booking: allergies, type of meal, occasion… The restaurant groups them in **categories**.

### Categories

```
GET    /api/v1/partnership/restaurants/:restaurant_id/special-options/categories/
POST   /api/v1/partnership/restaurants/:restaurant_id/special-options/categories/
GET    /api/v1/partnership/restaurants/:restaurant_id/special-options/categories/:id/
PATCH  /api/v1/partnership/restaurants/:restaurant_id/special-options/categories/:id/
DELETE /api/v1/partnership/restaurants/:restaurant_id/special-options/categories/:id/
```

```json
{
    "id": 2,
    "label": "Allergies",
    "description": "Tell us about your allergies.",
    "required": false,
    "unique_choice": false,
    "active": true,
    "options": [
        { "id": 7, "label": "Gluten", "message": null, "active": true }
    ]
}
```

| Field | Content |
|---|---|
| `label` | Up to 40 characters. Required at the creation |
| `description` | Up to 255 characters, or `null` |
| `required` | The booking widget of the restaurant requires a choice in this category. **Not enforced on your reservations** |
| `unique_choice` | One option at most can be chosen in this category |
| `active` | An inactive category and its options cannot be chosen |
| `options` | The options of the category, read-only. Change them with the routes below |

Filter the list with `?active=` and `?required=`, search with `?search=` (label), sort with `?ordering=id` or `label`.

### Options

```
GET    /api/v1/partnership/restaurants/:restaurant_id/special-options/
POST   /api/v1/partnership/restaurants/:restaurant_id/special-options/
GET    /api/v1/partnership/restaurants/:restaurant_id/special-options/:id/
PATCH  /api/v1/partnership/restaurants/:restaurant_id/special-options/:id/
DELETE /api/v1/partnership/restaurants/:restaurant_id/special-options/:id/
```

```json
{
    "id": 7,
    "category_id": 2,
    "category_label": "Allergies",
    "label": "Gluten",
    "message": null,
    "active": true
}
```

| Field | Content |
|---|---|
| `category_id` | Category of the option. Required at the creation, it cannot change: to move an option, create a new one |
| `label` | Up to 40 characters. Required at the creation |
| `message` | Message shown to the guest when the option is chosen, up to 255 characters, or `null` |
| `active` | An inactive option cannot be chosen |

Filter the list with `?category_id=` and `?active=`, search with `?search=` (label), sort with `?ordering=id`, `label` or `category`.

The creations accept an `Idempotency-Key` header. See [Retry Safely](/partnership-documentation/guides/05-manage-reservations#retry-safely).

### Deletion

`DELETE` answers `204` and hides the category or the option: it disappears from the lists and can no longer be chosen. The reservations that chose it keep it, with its label. The options of a deleted category disappear with it.

To pause a category or an option for a while, set `active` to `false` instead: it stays listed and can be activated again.

### Choose Options on a Reservation

Send `special_option_ids` when you [create](/partnership-documentation/guides/05-manage-reservations#create-a-reservation) or [update](/partnership-documentation/guides/05-manage-reservations#update-a-reservation) a reservation:

```json
{ "special_option_ids": [7, 12] }
```

- Each option must be active, in an active category. Otherwise: `400 VALIDATION_ERROR`.
- A category with `unique_choice` takes one option at most. Otherwise: `400 VALIDATION_ERROR`.
- The `required` categories are not enforced on your reservations.
- On a `PATCH`, the list replaces the options of the reservation. An empty list removes them. Without `special_option_ids`, they do not change.

## Opening Hours

The opening hours tell the guests when the restaurant is open. They are shown in the booking widget only: **they do not change the availabilities**, which come from the [shifts](#shifts) of the restaurant.

```
GET    /api/v1/partnership/restaurants/:restaurant_id/opening-hours/
POST   /api/v1/partnership/restaurants/:restaurant_id/opening-hours/
GET    /api/v1/partnership/restaurants/:restaurant_id/opening-hours/:id/
PATCH  /api/v1/partnership/restaurants/:restaurant_id/opening-hours/:id/
DELETE /api/v1/partnership/restaurants/:restaurant_id/opening-hours/:id/
```

```json
{ "id": 3, "weekday": 0, "from_hour": "11:30:00", "to_hour": "14:30:00" }
```

| Field | Content |
|---|---|
| `weekday` | Day of the week: `0` is Monday, `6` is Sunday |
| `from_hour` | Opening time, `HH:MM:SS` |
| `to_hour` | Closing time, `HH:MM:SS`. Must be after `from_hour` |

A day can have several ranges, e.g. lunch and dinner. The list is sorted by `weekday`, then `from_hour`. Filter it with `?weekday=`, sort it with `?ordering=weekday`, `from_hour` or `id`.

<Aside type="caution">
**Local time, not UTC.** The opening hours repeat every week: they are in the local time of the restaurant (see its `timezone`), unlike the other dates and times of the API. `19:00:00` stays 19:00 in summer and in winter.
</Aside>

- A range cannot go past midnight: `from_hour` must be before `to_hour`. For 19:00 to 01:00, create `19:00:00`–`23:59:59` on the first day and `00:00:00`–`01:00:00` on the next one.
- Two ranges of the same day cannot overlap.
- Otherwise: `400 VALIDATION_ERROR`, with a `message` that names the overlapping range.
- `DELETE` removes the range for good and answers `204`.
- The creations accept an `Idempotency-Key` header.

## Closed Days

Closed days are the exceptional closings of the restaurant: holidays, works, private events… No new reservation can be made on these days: the [availabilities](/partnership-documentation/guides/06-read-availabilities) list no slot, and Google is updated too.

```
GET    /api/v1/partnership/restaurants/:restaurant_id/closed-days/
POST   /api/v1/partnership/restaurants/:restaurant_id/closed-days/
GET    /api/v1/partnership/restaurants/:restaurant_id/closed-days/:id/
PATCH  /api/v1/partnership/restaurants/:restaurant_id/closed-days/:id/
DELETE /api/v1/partnership/restaurants/:restaurant_id/closed-days/:id/
```

```json
{ "id": 12, "from_day": "2026-12-24", "to_day": "2026-12-26" }
```

| Field | Content |
|---|---|
| `from_day` | First closed day, `YYYY-MM-DD`. Required |
| `to_day` | Last closed day, included, `YYYY-MM-DD`. Required. For a single day, set it to `from_day` |

The days are days of the restaurant calendar. The list is sorted by `from_day`; sort it with `?ordering=from_day`, `to_day` or `id`. Get the current and coming closings with `?to_day__gte=<today>`; `?from_day__lte=` is available too.

<Aside type="caution">
Closing days does not cancel the reservations already taken on these days: they stay. Read them with the [reservations](/partnership-documentation/guides/05-manage-reservations#list-reservations) filters, e.g. `?start_time__date__gte=2026-12-24&start_time__date__lte=2026-12-26`, and cancel them if needed.
</Aside>

- `from_day` must be before or on `to_day`.
- A closing cannot end before today (in the calendar of the restaurant): it would have no effect.
- Two closings cannot share a day: change the existing one instead.
- Otherwise: `400 VALIDATION_ERROR`, with a `message` that names the overlapping closing.
- `DELETE` removes the closing for good, opens the days again, and answers `204`. A past closing can be deleted.
- The creations accept an `Idempotency-Key` header.

## Shifts

Shifts are the services of the restaurant: lunch, dinner… They set **when guests can book**, for how many guests, and until when. The [availabilities](/partnership-documentation/guides/06-read-availabilities) come from them: a time with no shift has no slot.

```
GET    /api/v1/partnership/restaurants/:restaurant_id/shifts/
POST   /api/v1/partnership/restaurants/:restaurant_id/shifts/
GET    /api/v1/partnership/restaurants/:restaurant_id/shifts/:id/
PATCH  /api/v1/partnership/restaurants/:restaurant_id/shifts/:id/
DELETE /api/v1/partnership/restaurants/:restaurant_id/shifts/:id/
```

### Kinds of Shifts

| `kind` | What it is | Created with |
|---|---|---|
| `WEEKLY` | Repeats every week on its `weekday` | `weekday`, `from_hour`, `to_hour`, no `date` |
| `EXCEPTION` | Replaces a weekly shift on one `date`: other hours, other limits, or closed that day with `"active": false` | `date` and `replaces_shift_id` (a weekly shift) |
| `SPECIAL_OPENING` | Opens the restaurant on one `date`, without replacing a weekly shift | `date`, `from_hour`, `to_hour`, no `replaces_shift_id` |

The `kind` is read-only: it follows `date` and `replaces_shift_id` at the creation, and never changes.

### Fields

```json
{
    "id": 6,
    "kind": "WEEKLY",
    "date": null,
    "replaces_shift_id": null,
    "weekday": 3,
    "from_hour": "18:00:00",
    "to_hour": "21:30:00",
    "active": true,
    "interval": 30,
    "min_seats": 1,
    "max_seats": 16,
    "shift_limit_active": false,
    "shift_seats_limit": 16,
    "interval_limit_active": false,
    "interval_seats_limit": 1,
    "message": null,
    "show_message": false,
    "override_before_service": false,
    "duration_before_service": false,
    "reservation_before_duration": "01:00:00",
    "show_call_option": true,
    "show_call_option_on_limit_reached": true,
    "created_at": "2026-03-27T16:29:46.462481Z",
    "updated_at": "2026-03-27T16:29:46.462539Z"
}
```

| Field | Content |
|---|---|
| `kind` | `WEEKLY`, `EXCEPTION` or `SPECIAL_OPENING`. Read-only |
| `date` | Day of an exception or a special opening, `YYYY-MM-DD`. `null` for a weekly shift |
| `replaces_shift_id` | Weekly shift an exception replaces. `null` for the other kinds |
| `weekday` | `0` is Monday, `6` is Sunday. Given by the `date` for a dated shift |
| `from_hour`, `to_hour` | Start and end of the service, `HH:MM:SS`, local time. `from_hour` must be before `to_hour` |
| `active` | An inactive shift takes no booking. An inactive exception closes its weekly shift on that day |
| `interval` | Minutes between two bookable times: `15`, `30` or `60` |
| `min_seats`, `max_seats` | Smallest and biggest party size that can book. `min_seats` up to `max_seats` |
| `shift_limit_active`, `shift_seats_limit` | When active: at most this many guests over the whole shift |
| `interval_limit_active`, `interval_seats_limit` | When active: at most this many guests per bookable time |
| `message`, `show_message` | Message shown to the guests in the booking widget, up to 255 characters, when `show_message` is `true` |
| `override_before_service` | Use the booking deadline below instead of the one of the restaurant |
| `reservation_before_duration` | Booking deadline: bookings close this long before the time, as `HH:MM:SS` (`1 00:00:00` from 24 hours) |
| `duration_before_service` | The deadline counts from the start of the service, instead of from each bookable time |
| `show_call_option` | The booking widget offers to call the restaurant |
| `show_call_option_on_limit_reached` | The booking widget offers to call the restaurant when a limit is reached |

<Aside type="caution">
**Local time, not UTC.** Like the opening hours, `from_hour`, `to_hour`, `date` and `weekday` are in the local time of the restaurant (see its `timezone`). `created_at` and `updated_at` are in UTC.
</Aside>

### List Shifts

The list holds the weekly shifts and the dated shifts **from today** (in the calendar of the restaurant). With a date filter, it holds the past dated shifts too. A shift is always reachable by its `id`.

| Filter | Content |
|---|---|
| `?kind=` | `WEEKLY`, `EXCEPTION` or `SPECIAL_OPENING` |
| `?weekday=` | Day of the week |
| `?active=` | `true` or `false` |
| `?date=`, `?date__gte=`, `?date__lte=` | Day of the dated shifts, `YYYY-MM-DD` |

The list is sorted by `date`, `weekday`, then `from_hour`: the weekly shifts come after the dated ones. Sort it with `?ordering=date`, `weekday`, `from_hour` or `id`.

### Create a Shift

```json
POST /api/v1/partnership/restaurants/:restaurant_id/shifts/

{ "weekday": 4, "from_hour": "12:00:00", "to_hour": "14:30:00", "max_seats": 8 }
```

An exception only needs its `date` and the shift it replaces: the hours, the interval and the party sizes you do not send are taken from the replaced shift. To close a weekly shift on one day:

```json
{ "date": "2026-12-24", "replaces_shift_id": 6, "active": false }
```

A special opening needs its `date` and its hours:

```json
{ "date": "2026-12-27", "from_hour": "12:00:00", "to_hour": "15:00:00" }
```

The other fields take their default values. The creations accept an `Idempotency-Key` header.

### Rules

All of them answer `400 VALIDATION_ERROR`, with a `message`:

- `from_hour` must be before `to_hour`: a shift cannot go past midnight. Create one shift on each day instead.
- `min_seats` must be up to `max_seats`.
- Two weekly shifts of the same day cannot overlap.
- A dated shift cannot overlap the weekly shifts of its day, except the one it replaces, nor the other dated shifts of its date. The `message` names the overlapping shift.
- `replaces_shift_id` must be a weekly shift, and the `date` must be on its weekday. A `weekday` sent with a `date` must agree with it.
- A weekly shift has one exception per date at most.
- A `date` cannot be before today.
- `replaces_shift_id` needs a `date`.
- The `date` and `replaces_shift_id` of a shift cannot change, and a weekly shift cannot get a date: create a new shift instead.
- The `weekday` of a weekly shift with exceptions cannot change: their dates would no longer match.

### Delete a Shift

`DELETE` answers `204`.

- An exception or a special opening is removed: on its date, the weekly shifts apply again.
- A weekly shift disappears from the list and takes no more booking. If it has exceptions, they stay and keep replacing it on their dates.

<Aside type="note">
The [closed days](#closed-days) win over the shifts: no shift opens a closed day.
</Aside>
